Ready houses of the team districts: Blend Swap #92125 "Family House Collection" (Blender 2.79, uploaded 2018-07-17).

Source page: http://www.blendswap.com/blends/view/92125

Original file: houses_blendswap.blend (six modern family houses, each as a detailed model and as a game model
baked onto one trim sheet).
Adaptation (scripts/prepare_family_houses.py, frontend/scripts/pack-city-model.mjs): every house built from its
detailed model, the house and its windows, doors, garage doors, railings, chimneys and columns joined into one
mesh on the collection's own trim sheets (brick and stone, 1024 × 1024, JPEG), each house in both; the ambient
occlusion maps and the bump left off, the fifth house's glass mapped onto a pane of the sheet, a seed per window
for the city's night lights, packed to a self-contained meshopt GLB. Free for personal, educational and
commercial use. No remote runtime requests to the author.
