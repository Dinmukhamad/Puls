Author's description: a small-town station of the south of France, freely reproduced after the
JOUEF model kit "Gare de Neuvy".

Changes (scripts/prepare_railway_station.py, frontend/scripts/pack-city-model.mjs): the building's
outside only, without subdivision and bevels; doors, windows and shutters as plain boxes, benches as
seat, back and legs, the tiled roofs as plain slabs with gutters, rake trims and a ridge; interior,
track, platform, lamps, posters, plaques, handles, hinges, downpipes and one bench dropped; every
material a plain colour with no textures; the name boards read the names of the Puls cities instead
of "BLENDERVILLE" (DejaVu Sans Bold glyphs); sized to the city and packed to a meshopt GLB.
