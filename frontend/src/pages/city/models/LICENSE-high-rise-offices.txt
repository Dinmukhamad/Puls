The supplied City.blend contains nine building variants. They have been separated
into individual ground-centred glTF scenes, with their packed facade textures and
proportions preserved. Legacy shaders were converted, window markers added, and
the output meshopt-compressed for the Puls city. Cameras, source lighting and the
ground plane are excluded. No endorsement by the original author is implied.

Conversion: scripts/prepare_high_rise_offices.py and
frontend/scripts/pack-city-model.mjs. Models: high-rise-offices.glb.
