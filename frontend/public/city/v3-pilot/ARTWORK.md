# Puls CRM Harbour

Original geometry, composition and geometric lettering authored for this project.
No third-party art assets, fonts or textures are included in `crm-island.glb`.
The artwork follows the project's licensing terms.

The authoring script and technical notes are in `scripts/city-v3/`.
Three.js and meshoptimizer are software tools used by the pipeline, not external
art sources. Their own software licences continue to apply to those dependencies.
